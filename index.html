Settings

Aiming
Controller: the aim turns at a steady speed while you drag. Instant: it jumps straight to your finger.
Shooting
Tap: drag to aim, then tap to shoot. Tap or lift: lifting your finger after a drag also shoots.

How to play

Drag left or right to turn the aim, like a controller. Tap anywhere to shoot. You can change this in Settings.

Tap the Next bubble or Pip's feet to swap.

Match 3 or more of one colour to pop them. Anything left hanging falls for bonus points!

The ceiling drops when the sign reaches 0. Keep bubbles above the pearl line.
